O82 — MOV chain collapse and dead high-byte elimination
========================================================

Implementation: V6ClangPeephole.cpp
  - eliminateDeadMVI  (Pattern A)
  - collapseMovChain  (Pattern B)
  - isRegDeadAfter    (fixed: implicit-killed super-register artifact)

Bug fixes required during implementation:
  1. isRegDeadAfter incorrectly treated "implicit killed $hl" on "MOV A, L"
     as a genuine read of H.  Root cause: when L (sub-register of HL) is
     last-used, the RA adds "implicit killed $hl" as a liveness marker for
     the pair — this is NOT a read of H's value.  Fixed by detecting the
     pattern: if an implicit-killed super-register operand is present AND
     there exists an explicit USE of a disjoint sub-register of the same
     pair, the super-register kill is a liveness artifact, not a true read.

  2. collapseMovChain consumer detection used !ReadsX in the consumer check.
     The consumer itself reads X as its source operand, so ReadsX was always
     true at that point, preventing the chain from ever being collapsed.
     Fixed by checking for the consumer candidate BEFORE the "ReadsX → break"
     guard, and moving the intermediate-read bail-out after the candidate check.

  3. After rewriting the consumer (MOV Z, X → MOV Z, Y), stale
     "implicit killed $de" operands on the rewritten instruction confused the
     second isRegDeadAfter call that checked whether the producer could be
     erased.  Fixed by removing that second check entirely: given no
     intermediate reads of X (loop invariant) and X dead after the consumer
     (first check), the producer is provably a dead write.
